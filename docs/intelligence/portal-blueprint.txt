2. Portal Rules
Portal may expose technical diagnostics, but user-facing and engineering/admin views must be visually separated.
All forecasts display baseline, prediction horizon, generatedAt, dataThrough, uncertainty and model version where applicable.
Every chart has accessible tabular equivalent.
No model may be promoted from the portal without validation gates and audit record.
